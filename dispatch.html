Questions

My MC authority is brand new. Can you still help?

Yes. Some brokers only work with authorities that are a few months old, but many accept new carriers - we know which ones and set you up with them.

Do I have to take every load you find?

No. We send you the load details first and book it only after you say yes.

How and when do I pay the dispatch fee?

Only for loads we booked: 4% of the load rate for open trailers, 3% for enclosed. We send a simple invoice for the loads of the week.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. If it isn't working for you, you can stop at any time.

What do you need to start?

Your MC / USDOT number, a certificate of insurance (cargo and auto liability), a W-9 and your trailer details. We'll ask for them on the first call.
